Section 01 — Heritage
1959
A workshop in Makkah

Adada & Kabbani began in 1959 as a single joinery workshop in Makkah, making furniture and fittings by hand for the city's homes and institutions.

Six decades later that workshop has become a manufacturing house — but the founding measure still governs the work: every joint drawn, every reveal held, nothing left to the site. Detailed founder-to-global narrative to be drawn from the profile text.
