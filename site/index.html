09The Process

From drawing to installation.

Six stages. One house. One unbroken line.

  1. Design Concept and intent, drawn.
  2. Engineering Every junction resolved before a cut is made.
  3. Shop Drawings Dimensioned to the millimetre; nothing to chance.
  4. Manufacture Cut and built in our Makkah factory, to machine precision.
  5. Hand-Finishing Finished by hand; the touch machines cannot give.
  6. Install Delivered and fitted as one piece of thinking.
